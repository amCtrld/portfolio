# Synthesis

September brought a wave of unification across billing, inventory, and payables for BowlRMS, while on a personal level, I adopted a new mental framework for balanced decision-making.

## Company Development
- **Unified Billing & Subscriptions**: Migrated to a single payment provider supporting daily and weekly cycles, with consistent subscription expiry enforcement and a simplified renewal experience.
- **Inventory Precision & Menu Options**: Recipe-linked items now support fractional sales and flexible consumption timing. Introduced a dedicated Options workspace for building reusable, inventory-linked or preparation-only customization groups.
- **Supplier Payables & Analytics**: Closed the payables gap by turning restocks into Accounts Payable liabilities, complete with full ledger reconciliation, aging analytics, and detailed supplier payment tracking.

## Personal Development
- **The Triad Mental Model**: Developed a framework to avoid emotional choices: *Thesis* (purpose and action), *Antithesis* (friction and risk analysis), and *Synthesis* (merging both into actionable reality).
- **Embracing the Timeline**: Made peace with delaying my schooling to pursue other major upcoming goals for the company and myself.

## The Lessons
- **Stress-Testing is Essential**: The *Antithesis* isn't just complaining; bringing in the necessary friction and edge cases makes the final *Synthesis* much more robust and grounded.
- **Flexibility Enables Control**: Whether it's accommodating fractional inventory tracking or new billing cycles, giving users more granular flexibility ultimately leads to better accuracy.
