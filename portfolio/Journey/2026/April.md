# Quiet Iterations

Stepped back from the street and back into the codebase. Less pitching, more fixing.

## Company Development
- **BowlRMS Development**: Iterated and fixed various sections of BowlRMS. Slow, unglamorous work, but the platform kept getting sturdier.
- **Bidbee Registered**: Registered Bidbee as a business. Haven't worked on Bidbee itself for some time though, my sights are still fixed on BowlRMS.
- **Paused Outreach**: Paused going out to look for clients for a while to focus on the product.

## Personal Development
- **Pulled Back Deliberately**: Pausing outreach felt like retreat at first. With time, it felt like perhaps mother nature was saving me from signing up the wrong clients for an unfinished product.

## The Lessons
- **Focus Is a Decision**: Registering Bidbee while still choosing BowlRMS taught me that you can protect future options without splitting present focus.
- **Timing Protects You**: Not every closed door is a loss. Sometimes a pause saves you from premature pressure.
