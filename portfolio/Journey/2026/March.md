# Breaking Changes

Synching dev and prod the hard way. A month of going out, getting shut down, and still building.

## Company Development
- **BowlRMS Development**: Kept building BowlRMS through breaking changes while trying to sync dev and prod. I made the mistake of working directly on prod, which made every break feel heavier.
- **Client Outreach**: Went out looking for clients and pitching BowlRMS. Not much success, but every conversation returned useful feedback.

## Personal Development
- **Showing Up Anyway**: Getting told no in person hits different from getting ignored online. Still showed up, still pitched, still came back to build.

## The Lessons
- **Never Build on Prod**: Dev and prod exist for a reason. Working on prod turns small mistakes into breaking changes.
- **No Still Means Learn**: Client rejections stung, but they pointed at what to fix next in BowlRMS.
