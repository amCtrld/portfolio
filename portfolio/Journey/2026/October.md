# The Cost of Conviction

October brought a major BowlRMS finance overhaul and a difficult reset of my own timeline.

## Company Development
- **Finance Overhaul**: Improved traceability with food and beverage revenue splits, weighted-average inventory costing, movement history, and stocktakes. Payroll approvals, separate statutory liabilities, accounts payable, reconciliation, period close, exports, and AI finance answers also improved.
- **Subscriptions and Reservations**: Added a management page for plans, revenue, renewals, and expiries. Reservations now have configurable lead times and table holds, searchable references, and a clearer lifecycle from request to arrival or no-show.
- **Zawidi**: Started an AI gifting platform that recommends physical gifts or experiences from details about a person and occasion. Partner options and booking support are planned; it is still a work in progress.

## Personal Development
- **A Timeline Reset**: Being jobless and covering my bills has shifted my goals. Many jobs require a degree, and I need capital to market BowlRMS while sustaining myself. Belief in the product keeps me moving. Practicing the Triad is not easy, but I am on track.

## The Lessons
- **Trust Comes From Traceability**: Movement histories, approvals, liabilities, and reconciliation help explain how each number got there.
- **A Delayed Timeline Is Not a Lost Goal**: Circumstances change the pace, not the destination. The Triad helps me keep building.
