# The Valley

I had taught myself to choose and brace, but this month threw me off-balance in a not-so great a way. A month of discovery and decisions, developing frameworks to choose and brace, not just phrases.

## Company Development
- **BowlRMS Development**: I kept iterating various aspects of BowlRMS. Switched from Cloudinary to BunnyCDN for fast, more affordable and monitorable* CDN. The plan is to have the Platform fully opeartional when we go production.

## Personal Development

- **Bleeding Steel**: I took a punch in the gut and let us say, the best I could do is hold the plateau. Couldn't rise so I had to struggle to to fall. The number of times I considered smoking, or maybe even a drink...

## The Lessons

- **Frameworks over Slogans**: Choose and Brace was a slogan, and it didn't do much when it was tested. It wasn't a conviction, just a result of infliction. Frameworks act like algorithms serving as the default 'How to...'

- **Progression**: Looking at last month, and the plans I had when finishing the previous year, I do not think that I am making progress, as I should. And this is not the way of becoming I want.
